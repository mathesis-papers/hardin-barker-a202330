# A general formula for Hardin's binary arrays proving Barker's conjectures

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

We prove Hardin's empirical quartic and Barker's conjectures in the FORMULA field of OEIS A202330, revision 8 (27 May 2018), by counting the entire two-parameter array A202335: for n,m>=1 its entry is 2+2*binomial(n+m+2,n+1)+binomial(n+m,n). The row-chain proof also establishes the empirical polynomials and conjectural generating functions and recurrences in A202331--A202334, both Kotesovec diagonal recurrences in A202329, and the first two column identities. Every fixed-width sequence has minimal eventual recurrence order equal to its width plus one.
